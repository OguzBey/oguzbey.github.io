Rust to Riches: Car Dealer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Türkçe

In short

Rust to Riches: Car Dealer (in Turkish: Hurdadan Servete: Galerici) is a car-dealer game. There are no accounts or logins, and the game collects nothing that identifies you. To improve the game it sends anonymous usage statistics and error reports (you can turn them off in the game's Settings). To keep the game free it shows Google AdMob ads, and to show and measure those ads AdMob processes some information from your device. On iPhone and iPad your save can also be backed up to your own iCloud, and Game Center is optional.

What stays on your device

Your dealership's name, your save (cars, money, day, upgrades and progress), your settings (language, sound, vibration, graphics), which purchases you own and simple ad counters (for example how many rewarded ads you watched today, so the daily limits work) are stored on your device. None of this is sent to us.

Save backup

  • iPhone and iPad: so you can continue after changing devices or reinstalling, a compressed copy of your save (including your dealership's name, day, money and progress) is stored in your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). If iCloud holds a different, newer save, the game asks which one you want to continue. We can't see this backup; it is covered by Apple's privacy terms. You can turn iCloud off for the game under Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
  • Android: if Android's automatic backup is turned on, your save may be included in your Google account's device backup. We can't see this backup; it is covered by Google's privacy terms. You can manage it under Settings › Google › Backup.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one (for example to finish a repair instantly), and from time to time a full-screen ad when a day ends (never in the first days, and never after you buy "Remove ads"). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use:

  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped),
  • crash and performance data of the ad software,
  • and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone and iPad, advertising ID on Android).

Your choices:

  • iPhone and iPad: when you first open the game, before any ad software starts, Apple's tracking permission request asks whether the game may use your advertising identifier. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the identifier is not shared and the ads you see are less personalised; the game is otherwise the same. You can change this at any time under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form (Google UMP) is shown before ads start. You can change your choice in the game under Settings (⚙) › Ad privacy choices.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID under Settings › Privacy › Ads (on some phones Settings › Google › Ads).
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The game offers three optional one-time purchases: "Remove ads", "Boss pack" and "Special paints". They are handled by Apple (App Store) on iPhone and iPad and by Google Play on Android. We never see your payment details or your account. The game only remembers which items you own; "Restore purchases" in the game's Shop asks Apple or Google again.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

Game Center is optional. If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores (for example total profit or cars sold) are sent to Apple's Game Center service and may be visible to other players under your Game Center nickname, according to your Game Center settings. This is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out under Settings › Game Center.

Anonymous statistics (PostHog)

To improve the game we count, anonymously, how long sessions last and in-game steps: tutorial steps, the end of each day (game day, money, profit, cars sold), cars bought and sold (price, profit, car type), paint jobs, upgrades, loans, auctions and orders, achievements, ad and purchase results, the answer to the tracking-permission request, and whether you continued an iCloud save. This uses an identifier created at random on your device; it is not an advertising identifier and does not identify you. Along with it we send the device model, operating system, app version and the game's language. Your dealership's name, anything you type, your contact details and your advertising identifier are never sent.

If something goes wrong in the game, an anonymous error report is sent the same way so we can fix it: the error message and where in the game's code it happened.

The data is processed by PostHog on servers in the United States. As with any internet request, PostHog receives your IP address and derives an approximate location (country and city) from it; we use this only for statistics, for example to leave out the test devices of app store reviewers. The data is not used for advertising or tracking, is never sold and is kept for at most one year. You can turn statistics off at any time under Settings (⚙) › Anonymous statistics in the game; turning it off also deletes anything still waiting to be sent. The game can still be played offline.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights and deleting your data

We hold no account or personal data about you; anonymous statistics are linked only to a random identifier on your device and are deleted after at most one year. To stop them, turn off Settings › Anonymous statistics. To delete the game's data from your device, delete the game; "New game" in the game's Settings also erases your progress and replaces the iCloud copy. To stop the iCloud backup, turn iCloud off for the game (see above); to stop the Android backup, turn off backup in your phone's Google settings. You can turn off the advertising identifier on iPhone under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and on Android under Settings › Privacy › Ads. For questions under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, KVKK or similar laws, write to us at the address below.
