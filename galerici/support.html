Frequently asked

My purchase is gone. In the game, tap the gear (⚙, top right) › Shop › "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my dealership? On iPhone and iPad your save is backed up to your iCloud: sign in with the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, install the game, and it asks whether to continue the save from iCloud. On Android, restore your phone from your Google backup when setting it up. Otherwise saves stay on the device, and deleting the game deletes the save.

I want to change my ad consent. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: gear (⚙) › "Ad privacy choices". On iPhone you can also turn tracking off under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Are there many ads? Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch one. A full-screen ad can appear at the end of some days, never in the first days and not too often. "Remove ads" in the Shop removes those end-of-day ads for good; rewarded ads stay available if you want them.

Achievements and leaderboards. On iPhone and iPad they use Game Center. Sign in under Settings › Game Center; it is optional.

Language, sound, dealership name. All in the game's Settings (⚙).
