Rust to Riches: Car Dealer

Hurdadan Servete: Galerici · iPhone, iPad and Android · English and Turkish

Buy, fix, paint, haggle

A car dealer simulator: buy used cars and scrap wrecks, inspect them for accident records and liens, haggle with sellers, repair and paint them in your garage, then sell them for a profit. Grow from a corner garage to a car lot, a showroom and an auto plaza with auctions, customer orders, market trends and bank loans.

Free, with optional rewarded ads and a few one-time purchases. Plays offline.
